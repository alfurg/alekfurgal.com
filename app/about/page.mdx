I help founders, marketing leads, and SaaS teams create content that builds trust around technical products. That means closing the gap between how a business sees itself and how its website makes that value clear to the people it needs to reach.

I started my career in agency SEO, working on technical health, site structure, and content strategy across different clients and industries. 
That gave me a practical understanding of how search visibility is built: not only through keywords or individual pages, but through technical health, coherence, and authority.

That foundation still shapes how I write. Once someone lands on a page, visibility has done its first job. What matters next is whether the page makes a clear argument, clarifies the product, earns trust, and supports the larger system it belongs to.

Most of my writing has been for technical and SaaS audiences. At a software house, I wrote for founders, marketers, and decision-makers building digital products and the businesses around them. 
Later, at Surfshark, I created and improved content across cybersecurity, networking, and digital products — a competitive SaaS space where vague explanations do not hold up, and trust depends on substance, accuracy, and real product understanding.
